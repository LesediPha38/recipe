 <h1>Simple Omelette Recipe </h1>
 <p>An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal.This classic omelette combines beaten eggscooked for perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or 
 meats.</p>
<br>
<table>
  <h3>Preparation time</h3>
 <ul><b>Total:</b>Approxiamtely 10 minutes</ul>
 <ul><b>Preparation:</b>5 minutes</ul>
 <ul><b>Cooking:</b>5 minutes</ul>
</table>
<br>
 <h2>Ingredients</h2>
 <ul>2-3 large eggs</ul>
 <ul>Salt, to taste</ul>
 <ul>Pepper, to taste</ul>
 <ul>1 tablespoon of butter or oil </ul>
 <ul>Optional fillings:cheese,diced vegetables,cooked meats,herbs</ul>
