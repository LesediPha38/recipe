Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal.This classic omelette combines beaten eggscooked for perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.


Preparation time

    Total:Approxiamtely 10 minutes
    Preparation:5 minutes
    Cooking:5 minutes

Ingredients

    2-3 large eggs
    Salt, to taste
    Pepper, to taste
    1 tablespoon of butter or oil
    Optional fillings:cheese,diced vegetables,cooked meats,herbs
